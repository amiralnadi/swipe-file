## Brand Content

### Solana Docu-Style Founder Content Series
- **Link:** https://x.com/solana/status/2050183461248381283
- **Tags:** #documentary #brand #founder #authenticity #community #tiktok
- **Description:** Solana's docu-style content series featuring founders and builders in the ecosystem. Uses authentic storytelling and behind-the-scenes moments to build community connection rather than product-focused messaging.
Why saving: Shows how established platforms use intimate, narrative-driven content to create parasocial connection with audience. Demonstrates that TikTok virality isn't about product demos — it's about making people feel like they're inside a story. Pattern applicable to Ouracle/Pairing positioning as identity-driven, not clinical.
When to use: Creating TikTok-first brand content that humanizes the platform, building founder narrative around your product, or designing content strategy that prioritizes emotional connection over feature selling.
- **Added:** 2026-05-06
